Optional: put a namespace into its own VPC (CIDRs may overlap across VPCs):

  kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
  apiVersion: network.hamid-cni.io/v1alpha1
  kind: VPC
  metadata:
    name: vpc-blue
  spec:
    cidr: 10.0.0.0/16
    vxlanID: 100
  EOF

  kubectl annotate ns <namespace> network.hamid-cni.io/vpc=vpc-blue --overwrite

Verify agents:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get ds -l app.kubernetes.io/name=hamid-cni

See examples/vpcs.yaml for an overlapping-CIDR demo.
